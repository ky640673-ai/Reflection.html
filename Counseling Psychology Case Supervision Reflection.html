Presented by : yogesh k25BPY034

Reflection on Counseling Psychology Case Supervision

🎬

In this session I presented a case of a male with paranoid schizophrenia based on a video of a psychiatric interview. I was very interested in a lot of the interview, especially in his explanations of his beliefs and experiences. He had delusions, heard voices and used odd words like 'pedentry.' We also talked about his cannabis use and whether it could be linked to his symptoms. It made me realize that knowing a case isn’t just watching the interview and working out the symptoms.

🔍

We discussed how to distinguish schizophrenia from substance-induced psychosis

We talked about how to differentiate between schizophrenia and substance induced psychosis and the importance of the person’s history. We also talked about the problem of working with someone who thinks they don't need treatment. In this discussion I learned something new about the LEAP approach. I learned that arguing with a client about what they believe in may not help, and that listening to them is important. We also discussed safety when working with clients who may have violent behavior or command hallucinations.

💡

The session gave me an insight into how case discussions happen and how there are many factors to be thought about before one can come to a conclusion. I have a lot to learn about how to handle such cases, but this talk helped me to understand the practical side of what we study in psychology.
